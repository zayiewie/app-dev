# The Summer I Turned Pretty
## Series Overview
### Details
**The Summer I Turned Pretty**

*Belly finds herself caught in an emotional tug-of-war between the complex, brooding Conrad and his warm, affectionate younger brother, Jeremiah.*

> Coming-of-age, romantic drama, and young adult (YA) genres

1. Why I Like It
- It is about young people around my age, which makes me feel relatable, plus the romance and drama give me butterflies!

2. Favorite Characters
- Conrad Fisher, Taylor Jewel, and Steven Conklin

3. Setting
- Cousins Beach, a gorgeous coastal getaway where the main characters spend every summer, creating a nostalgic atmosphere full of beach bonfires, parties, and long-lasting memories.

4. Rating: 9/10
- The song choices in the series are so beautiful
- the characters are on point
- The series is easy to understand the plot but also unpredictable which makes you feel more excited
- it takes too long for new episodes to be uploaded!
